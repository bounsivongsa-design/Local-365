Here is some minimum info I think should be on the Business Sign-up Form; feel free to add anything I may have missed. Obviously only items matching the business subscription will be visible on the site.
Business Name - Required
Business Description -  Required
Business Logo - Optional
Business Hours - Optional
Residential / Commercial Select one or both -  Required
Website URL - Optional
Social Media URL's - Optional
Business Owners Name -  Required
Email Address -  Required
Business Address - Optional
Phone Number -  Required
Business/Physical Address -  Required  "Zip Code is Required to be displayed on profile, Actual full address visible on profile is optional"
LLC Yes/No - Optional
Insured Yes/No - Optional
Veteran Yes/No - Optional - Veterans will receive a Veteran Badge on profile listings.
Established Year -  Required
Categories Wanted to be listed in: - Required  Check up to however many categories allowed per subscription plan (Bronze, Silver, Gold).
Business Service Keywords/Tags for search engine upto 250 characters option ie.. Electric, Electrical, Electrician, Local, Veteran, Hometown
